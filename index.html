UTC Time Zone Converter

Convert UTC or ISO timestamps to worldwide regional time zones with automatic daylight-saving handling.

1. Enter date & time

Use the date/time field for a UTC value, or paste a complete ISO timestamp containing Z or a UTC offset.
Examples: 2026-08-31T03:28:18Z or 2026-08-31T09:28:18.803+0600

2. Select destination region

Search by city or IANA region, such as Dhaka, Kathmandu, London, Tokyo, Sydney, or New York.
